Alzheimer's disease in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia: Current perspectives and genetic insights

Peer-reviewed reference: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39994993/

The 2025 review summarizes Saudi Alzheimer's disease perspectives and genetic evidence, and it motivates further population-specific research. The application uses it for regional context and the need for Saudi-cohort evaluation, not as validation of NeuroAPS-Net.
