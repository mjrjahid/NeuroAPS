Dementia prevalence within the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia National Guard Health System (2015–2023)

Peer-reviewed reference: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39962762/

The study examined NGHS participants aged 50 or older who sought medical care during 2015–2023. Dementia was identified in 3.37% of that defined population. This is not a Saudi general-population prevalence estimate and is not Dammam-specific.

Application use: Saudi regional context and motivation for locally representative evaluation. It is not evidence of NeuroAPS validation, deployment, or adoption.
