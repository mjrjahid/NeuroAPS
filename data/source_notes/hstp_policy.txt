- disease prevention as a stated program priority;
- published implementation evidence in the 2024 report.

Claim boundary:
The public HSTP material supports disease prevention. The exact wording “from treatment-based care toward prevention and early intervention” is not treated as a direct HSTP quotation. NeuroAPS uses early intervention only as a qualified application interpretation: a lightweight research workflow may support earlier review after local validation, governance approval, and clinician confirmation.

Application relevance:
The program provides national context for evaluating a resource-efficient neuroimaging research workflow. The application does not claim HSTP adoption, an SFDA pathway, hospital integration, or clinical deployment.
