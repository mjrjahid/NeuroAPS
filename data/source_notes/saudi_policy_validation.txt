Title: Saudi Public-Domain Policy, Health-Data, and Research Source Register
Validated: 29 August 2026
Purpose: Source register for the Policy Alignment and Evidence Library workspaces.

OFFICIAL GOVERNMENT SOURCES

1. Health Sector Transformation Program (HSTP)
Authority: Saudi Vision 2030
Official page: https://www.vision2030.gov.sa/en/explore/programs/health-sector-transformation-program
Official report: https://www.vision2030.gov.sa/media/h0yb5d03/health-sector-transformation-report-2024.pdf
Validated support: healthcare access, quality, service-model transformation, disease prevention, and digital transformation.
Application translation: resource-efficient MRI decision support can be evaluated as a prevention-aligned digital-health access and quality use case. “Early intervention” is a qualified application interpretation, not a direct HSTP quotation or proof of an adopted screening program.

2. National Strategy for Data & Artificial Intelligence (NSDAI)
Authority: Saudi Data & Artificial Intelligence Authority (SDAIA)
Official page: https://sdaia.gov.sa/en/SDAIA/SdaiaStrategies/Pages/NationalStrategyForDataAndAI.aspx
Official PDF: https://sdaia.gov.sa/en/SDAIA/SdaiaStrategies/Documents/NSDAI.pdf
Validated support: healthcare is a priority sector; the strategy connects data and AI with access to health services and preventive care.
Application translation: the application links research evidence, model context, governance, and human review to national data/AI priorities.

3. Seha Virtual Hospital and Innovation Empowerment Center
Authority: Saudi Ministry of Health
Official link: https://www.moh.gov.sa/en/ministry/projects/pages/seha-virtual-hospital.aspx
Validated support: more than 242 supported hospitals, 48 main specialties, 68 sub-specialties, annual operational capacity above 597,000 beneficiaries, teleradiology, an innovation sandbox, and a clinical research center.
Application translation: this is a relevant public evaluation pathway. It does not indicate partnership, regulatory approval, or completed integration.

4. Sections of MOH Statistics
Authority: Saudi Ministry of Health
Official link: https://www.moh.gov.sa/en/statistics/pages/dashboard.aspx
Validated support: an official gateway for hospital visits and admissions, chronic and communicable disease indicators, performance and quality, workforce, facilities, and preventive/therapeutic services.
Scope note: the dashboard overview does not establish a single definitive national or Dammam Alzheimer’s prevalence count.

5. National Data Governance Platform and PDPL Implementing Regulations
Authority: SDAIA
Platform: https://dgp.sdaia.gov.sa/wps/portal/pdp/home
Implementing Regulations: https://dgp.sdaia.gov.sa/wps/portal/pdp/knowledgecenter/details/PDPL2/
Validated support: Article 25 impact-assessment triggers, Article 26 health-data safeguards and compatible-purpose controls, and Article 33 processing records.
Application translation: documented lawful basis, purpose limitation, data minimization, impact assessment where applicable, access control, and auditability.

6. AI Ethics Self-Assessment
Authority: SDAIA National Data Governance Platform
Official link: https://dgp.sdaia.gov.sa/wps/portal/pdp/services/servicesdetails/AIEthicsAssessment/
Validated support: structured analysis of alignment with Saudi AI ethics principles.
Application translation: documented human oversight, transparency, reliability, and pre-deployment ethical review. Completing the self-assessment is not certification.

OFFICIAL RESEARCH DATASET

7. Alzheimer’s Disease Neuroimaging Initiative (ADNI)
Publisher: ADNI / Laboratory of Neuro Imaging (LONI)
Official portal: https://adni.loni.usc.edu/
Data and Samples: https://adni.loni.usc.edu/data-samples/
Validated support: approved researchers can request controlled access to ADNI imaging and related participant data through the secure IDA repository under ADNI data-use terms.
Scope note: ADNI is not labelled as unrestricted public-domain data, and this package does not redistribute the full ADNI dataset.

PUBLIC BACKGROUND

8. Saudipedia NSDAI overview
Publisher: Saudipedia / Saudi Ministry of Media
Public link: https://saudipedia.com/en/national-strategy-for-data-and-ai-nsdai
Use: background for NSDAI components, targets, initiatives, and priority sectors. The official SDAIA page and PDF remain the primary policy sources.

9. Regulations.AI NSDAI index
Publisher: Regulations.AI
Public link: https://regulations.ai/regulations/saudi-arabia-2020-10-national-strategy-data-ai
Use: secondary plain-language background only. The page states that it is not legal advice and should be verified against official text.

SCOPE

- Government sources, controlled research data, public background, and public research are labelled separately. Secondary indexes are not presented as law or regulation.
- This register supports policy and contextual alignment.
- It is not evidence of regulatory approval, clinical validation, hospital integration, or partnership.
- Public links should be rechecked before a formal submission or deployment review.
