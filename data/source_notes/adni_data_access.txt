Title: Alzheimer’s Disease Neuroimaging Initiative (ADNI) data access
Publisher: ADNI / Laboratory of Neuro Imaging (LONI)
Validated: 29 August 2026

Official portal:
https://adni.loni.usc.edu/

Data and Samples:
https://adni.loni.usc.edu/data-samples/

Validated support:
- ADNI imaging, clinical, genomic, biomarker, and related participant data are shared for research through the LONI Image and Data Archive.
- Researchers request access and accept the applicable ADNI data-use terms.
- The NeuroAPS paper reports an ADNI-2DPC cohort of 1,000 subjects: 500 AD and 500 CN.

Application boundary:
The Readiness workspace describes ADNI as controlled research access, not unrestricted public-domain data. The 10 packaged examples support local visualization; they are not the complete ADNI cohort and do not provide independent clinical validation.
